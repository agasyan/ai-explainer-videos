## Notes for the script

- Say "by 1914" or "1913", not "1912": no authoritative source supports 1912 (c1, c2).
- 1947: the departure airfield (Stephenville or Gander), the flight time, and the commander's name conflict across sources, so the video says only "September 1947" and "Newfoundland to England" (c4, c5).
- Do not say the autopilot takes off, or that most planes land themselves (c15, c16). Do not give an exact number of hand-flying minutes (c17).
- AF447: ice did not cause the crash alone; the BEA describes a chain (c21 to c23). The 2013 FAA rule came from the 2009 Colgan Air crash and a 2010 law, not from AF447, so the video does not link them.
- SAFO 13002 is voluntary guidance, not an order (c26).
- METR's 2025 slowdown is outdated by METR's own update (c33). The video uses only the perception gap, labelled as one 2025 study (c32).
- Anthropic: the gap is 50% versus 67%, not "17% worse" (c34). Small sample, one-hour task, preprint.
- DORA and the Microsoft survey are associations, not causes (c38, c44).
- Automation bias is not fixed by training alone (c47): the video says fundamentals let you check the output, not that they make you immune.
